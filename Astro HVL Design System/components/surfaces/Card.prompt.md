The base surface panel — wraps projects, team members, sponsors, stats, and content blocks.

```jsx
<Card interactive>
  <span className="eyebrow">Project</span>
  <h3>Hybrid Demonstrator</h3>
  <p>First student hybrid motor to static fire in Bergen.</p>
</Card>
```

Props: `padded` (default true), `interactive` (hover lift + brand border), `flush` (no shadow), `as`. Compose freely with `.eyebrow`, headings, `Stat`, `Badge`, `Button`.
