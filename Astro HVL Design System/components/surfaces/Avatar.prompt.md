Circular avatar for team and crew — uses a photo when given, otherwise mono initials on navy.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Ada Lovelace" size="lg" ring />
<Avatar name="Kjell Nordmann" src="/team/kjell.jpg" />
```

Props: `name`, `src`, `size` (sm/md/lg/xl or px number), `ring` (brand ring). Initials derive from first + last name.
