We're putting together the first launch report. It lands here as soon as the
photos are sorted and the flight computer data has been reviewed.

## What to expect

Every report covers the same ground:

- **The rocket** — what we built, and what was new since last time.
- **The campaign** — preparations, the weather, and how the day played out.
- **The flight** — altitude, velocity and data from the flight computer.
- **Lessons** — what didn't go to plan, and what we change next time.

Stay tuned — or [join ASTRO](/join) and be there for the next one.
